About Time — bus sprite browser

All seven bus PNGs in pictures/, at native size and at 6× so the shape reads. Roles reflect the current wiring in BUS_VARIANT_BY_FRAME and the two calibration anchors.

Proposed two-phase animation. Phase 1 uses the smaller scale (bus0001 anchor near the house) and drives the bus along the back road, disappearing Z-behind front-hill. 10 game-minute gap. Phase 2 has the bus reappear on the front bend at the larger scale and animate to bus-parked-large at the school kerb. Use this browser to pick which sprites belong to which phase.
